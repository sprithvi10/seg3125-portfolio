How I Work

Process & Approach

I'm currently learning UI/UX design principles through SEG3125 at the University of Ottawa. My engineering background shapes how I approach design — systematically, iteratively, and with the user always in mind.

01

Understand

I begin by exploring user needs, context, interface requirements and constraints before touching a pixel of design.

02

Sketch & Structure

I create rough layout ideas on paper or digital tools like Figma, applying visual communication principles, such as hierarchy, balance, contrast, and scale.

03

Build & Refine

I implement with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap. I iterate based on feedback and test across devices for accessibility and usability.

04

Review & Learn

I participate in design critique sessions. I apply heuristic evaluation frameworks to analyse and improve both my work and others'.

📚 Currently learning through: Bootstrap 5, NN/g Articles, and the SEG3125 course materials at uOttawa.
